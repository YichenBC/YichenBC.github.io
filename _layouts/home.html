About

During my Ph.D., I studied the safety and alignment of large language models in Xiaoyun Wang’s group at Tsinghua.

I later explored agent safety, but found that agents still struggled to complete many tasks. That led me to agent training. My recent work spans GUI agents, visual instruction following, capability transfer after post-training, and long video generation.

You can reach me at gongyc18@gmail.com.
